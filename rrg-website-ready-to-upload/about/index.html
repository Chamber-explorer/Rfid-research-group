About RFID Research Group

RFID Research Group designs, manufactures and sells RFID and NFC security research hardware and accessories.

Company overview

RFID Research Group (RRG) designs, manufactures and sells RFID and NFC security research hardware and accessories. RRG was founded in 2018. Its hardware includes the Proxmark3 RDV4, of which more than 30,000 units have been sold, and the Proxmark5 Iceman Edition.

RRG also delivered the ChameleonUltra and USBNinja, and publishes open source firmware and tools on GitHub, including the Iceman fork of the Proxmark3 firmware and the ChameleonUltra firmware.

Mission

To provide security professionals and researchers with the most advanced, reliable and innovative tools for understanding and improving RFID and NFC security systems.

Engineering approach

RRG builds modular hardware around open firmware. Proxmark5 pairs an AT32F435 processor with a GOWIN FPGA and an open source FPGA toolchain direction, and its firmware is developed in the open with the community.
