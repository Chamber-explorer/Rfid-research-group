About RFID Research Group

RFID Research Group designs, manufactures and sells RFID and NFC security research hardware and accessories.

Company overview

RFID Research Group (RRG) develops hardware and open-source tools for RFID and NFC security research. Our work brings together hardware engineering, firmware development, and the security research community.

Mission

To develop practical, reliable, and accessible tools that support RFID and NFC security research.

Company history

  1. 2018

    RRG founded. Proxmark3 RDV4

    RRG was founded and launched the Proxmark3 RDV4 on Kickstarter.

    Proxmark3 RDV4

  2. 2018 to 2025

    More than 30,000 Proxmark3 RDV4 units sold

    More than 30,000 Proxmark3 RDV4 units were sold worldwide. RRG also delivered the ChameleonUltra and USBNinja, and hosts the Iceman firmware fork on GitHub.

    RRG on GitHub

  3. 2026-05

    Proxmark5 Iceman Edition launched on Indiegogo

    RRG launched the Proxmark5 Iceman Edition on Indiegogo in May 2026. The campaign raised more than US$1,000,000, reaching US$1,036,645 from 3,036 backers in 31 days.

    Proxmark5 on Indiegogo

  4. 2026

    Proxmark5 on sale through authorized distributors

    The Proxmark5 went on general sale through authorized distributors worldwide.

Engineering approach

RRG builds modular hardware around open firmware. Proxmark5 pairs an AT32F435 processor with a GOWIN FPGA and an open source FPGA toolchain direction, and its firmware is developed in the open with the community.
