Research

Research and open firmware

Hardware research and community-driven open firmware from RFID Research Group.

Hardware research

RRG designs RFID and NFC research hardware. Proxmark5 covers LF (125 kHz to 500 kHz), HF (13.56 MHz) and UHF (860 to 960 MHz) on one platform, with a 10-bit ADC for signal capture and a GOWIN FPGA for flexible logic.

Proxmark5 technical specifications

Community-driven open firmware

RRG hosts the Iceman fork of the Proxmark3 firmware and client on GitHub. Proxmark5 is compatible with the Iceman fork.

The firmware, the tooling and the roadmap are developed in the open.

github.com/rfidresearchgroup

Repositories

A selection of RRG public repositories on GitHub. The full list is on the RRG GitHub page.

  • proxmark3

    Iceman fork of Proxmark3. Firmware and client software.

  • ProxmarkStudio

    A cross-platform Proxmark GUI built on Tauri 2.0, with support for Windows, Linux, macOS, Android and iOS.

  • Proxmark5_BWM_esp32

    The BLE and WiFi module based on ESP32C2 for Proxmark5.

  • Proxmark5_FlipperZero_FAP

    An app that lets a Flipper Zero operate the Proxmark5.

  • ChameleonUltra

    Firmware for the Chameleon Ultra, based on the NRF52840, for card emulation, reading, writing and decrypting.

  • ChameleonUltraDocs

    Documentation for the Chameleon Ultra.

  • RFIDtools

    RRG Android app for use with Proxmark3 RDV4 and the Blueshark add-on.

  • homebrew-proxmark3

    Homebrew tap containing the Proxmark3 software and firmware.

  • mayhem-firmware

    Custom firmware for the HackRF with PortaPack H1, H2 and H4.
